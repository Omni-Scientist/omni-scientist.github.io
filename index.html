Seismology · three-component signal

A fifth of the discard pile was never noise

21.7%163 of 750 noise-labelled traces carry coherent transients
See the full finding →
  • The detector never sees the label. Threshold calibrated on IAAFT surrogates of the noise itself at a 1% false-alarm rate, measured back at 1.07%.
  • An amplitude-only detector at the same rate flags 2.0%: polarization and cross-channel coincidence carry the finding.
  • Stable at 19 to 25% across a fifty-fold threshold range and three null models.
Eight traces filed as noise. Lower half: the four with the strongest impulsive onsets in this sample.
Cardiology · audio

The metadata predicted the patient until the hospital changed

0.60 → 0.35metadata-only AUC, pooled then leave-one-cohort-out
See the full finding →
  • Below chance once the cohort is held out: the shortcut flips sign across sites. Drop 0.248, p < 0.0001.
  • The murmur-band acoustic feature barely moves: 0.701 to 0.656, not significant.
  • What sounded like a model of the heart was partly a model of the recording booth.
Heart-sound recordings, labelled normal or abnormal, grouped by the cohort that recorded them.
Astronomy · imaging

Change the telescope and its reading of the galaxy stays put

83.8%agreement on the same galaxies across two surveys
  • Chance agreement for this label mix is 35.0%; Cohen's κ is 0.75, CI 0.64 to 0.85.
  • Permutation test over the pairing gives p = 0.0002.
  • 105 galaxies, each imaged twice: once by SDSS, once by DECaLS.
Top row SDSS, bottom row DECaLS. Labels are the catalogue morphology.
Materials informatics · table

The usual split flattered the model by a factor of seven

3.1 - 7.0×error gap, random split versus leave-one-family-out
  • Cuprates: RMSE 12.1 → 51.6 K. Iron-based: 6.6 → 20.5 K. Other families: 4.3 → 30.3 K.
  • Same data, same model. Only the question changed, from interpolating inside a family to predicting one it had never seen.
Figure produced by the run itself.
Remote sensing · imaging

Rotate the patches and the benchmark falls apart

62.0% → 50.7%orientation-only classifier, upright then rotated
  • Features carry no colour and no raw pixels, only orientation histograms, angular FFT and coherence. Chance is 20%.
  • Per class the collapse is uneven: forest recall 0.68 → 0.27, residential 0.91 → 0.62, annual crop does not move.
  • A frame-locked cue was doing part of the work that looked like land-cover understanding.
Sentinel-2 land-cover patches, six of the benchmark classes.
